Runtime treatment (see `scripts/ui/main_menu.gd`): the roof/parapet slabs above 6.3 m are
dropped so the orbit camera stays inside the workshop, all meshes are merged into one `ArrayMesh`

night-studio palette, and the car sits on the raised turntable (y ≈ 0.19 m).
